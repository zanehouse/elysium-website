The Tools

The tools compute things like gamma exposure, implied volatility surfaces, and Black-Scholes greeks from prefetched data rather than a live feed. They're built carefully and tested, though they are research aids and not execution systems. Anyone trading off a number shown here should verify it against their own broker or data provider first, because the figures are refreshed on a schedule and are not exchange-certified.
