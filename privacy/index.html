Privacy

What this site collects, which is close to nothing.

No Analytics, No Cookies

Elysium runs no analytics. There is no Google Analytics, no Plausible, no cookie banner, and no tracking pixel on any page of this site. Nothing here sets a cookie, writes to local storage, or builds a visitor list; if someone asked me who read a particular report, I genuinely could not tell them.

There are also no forms. The site never asks for a name, an email, or a payment detail, because there is nowhere on it to enter one.

What Still Sees Traffic

The site is static, though it isn't sealed off. Two services receive a request from the visitor's browser when a page loads, and by the nature of how the web works each of those requests carries an IP address and a browser user agent:

  • jsDelivr, which serves the Chart.js and Three.js libraries behind the tools pages.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the site and keeps ordinary server logs.

Each of those handles that data under its own privacy policy, none of which I control and none of which reports anything back to me. I see no dashboard, no traffic numbers, and no per-visitor record from any of them.

Email and Direct Contact

Email sent to research@elysiumlab.markets or zane@elysiumlab.markets arrives in an ordinary inbox. I keep what gets sent to me so I can reply and refer back to the conversation later, though I don't add anyone to a mailing list, and I don't sell, rent, or share contact details with anyone.

The same applies to a call or text placed through the contact card: it stays between the sender and me.

Links Off the Site

Research reports link out to public filings, analyst notes, and news coverage, and the Links page points to X and Discord. Once a visitor follows one of those links, that destination's own privacy policy takes over. I have no visibility into what happens there.

Changes

If any of this changes, say I add analytics or start a newsletter, I'll update this page and the date below before the change goes live rather than after it. The point of publishing a policy this specific is that it stays accurate.
