That paragraph sits in the footer of every page on this site. The sections below explain what it means for someone actually reading a report.

Ratings and Price Targets

Reports carry a rating of Bullish, Neutral, or Bearish, and usually a price target over a stated horizon, most often 12 to 24 months. Those are opinions rather than predictions, and they are anchored to the data available on the publication date printed at the top of each report.

A rating is not a recommendation to buy or sell, and reports are not updated on a fixed schedule; a call published six months ago may not reflect what I think now. Anyone reading an older report should check the date first and treat the thesis as a snapshot of that moment.

Positions and Independence

I may hold positions in the securities I write about, including before and after publishing a report on them. Individual reports carry their own disclaimer noting this where it applies.

I don't manage outside capital, I don't offer advisory services, I don't accept sponsorships, and nobody pays me to cover a name or to reach a particular conclusion about one. That's the whole reason the research can stay independent, and it's worth stating plainly rather than burying.

Not Personalized Advice

Nothing published here accounts for any individual's financial situation, tax position, time horizon, or tolerance for risk, because I don't know any of those things about the person reading it. Most readers should treat this research as one input among several and speak with a licensed financial advisor before acting on it.

Risk

Equities, options, and leveraged products can lose value quickly, and options in particular can expire worthless. Past performance says nothing reliable about future results, and a thesis that worked once is not evidence the next one will.

Anyone acting on this research does so at their own risk and with their own capital. Losses that follow are theirs, which is the honest version of how this works.

Data Sources

Market and macro data on the tools pages comes from CBOE, FRED, the SEC, and the House Clerk, fetched on a schedule and committed to this site rather than streamed live. It is not real-time, it is not exchange-certified, and it should never be used for execution.

Figures inside research reports come from public filings, analyst reports, and press releases as of the date noted in each one. Sources get cited in the reports themselves so the work can be checked rather than taken on trust.
