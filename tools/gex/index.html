Gamma Exposure

Net dealer gamma by strike. Contract selection runs on its own, pulling the expirations and strikes that carry real open interest, then locating the zero-gamma flip and the gamma walls. Any optionable ticker is searchable; prefetched symbols load instantly, and anything else is looked up live.

Convention: dollar gamma per 1% move, computed per contract as Γ × weight × 100 × Spot² × 0.01, with calls contributing positive gamma and puts negative (the naive dealer-positioning assumption). Weight defaults to open interest, which settles overnight and is the standard, though the Weight by toggle switches it to today's traded volume; volume-weighting reflects the day's flow rather than resting positioning. Bars show net gamma by strike, green for positive and red for negative. The line is the cumulative profile, and the zero-gamma flip is where that line crosses zero nearest spot. The call wall and put wall are the strikes carrying the largest positive and largest negative net gamma. The Expiration dropdown defaults to the contracts that carry the most weight: 0DTE, every expiration within 30 days, and the next monthly OPEX. "All", a date range, or any single expiration out to LEAPS are all selectable. Strikes are always held within ±12% of spot, kept above a per-symbol open-interest floor, and required to have traded at least one contract today. That last filter drops strikes holding stale open interest nothing has touched today, which would otherwise stretch the strike axis out with little to show for it. The Snapshot toggle switches between the latest intraday chain and a frozen end-of-day close snapshot, available for prefetched symbols only. Prefetched symbols cover roughly 60 liquid indices, ETFs, and megacaps; they load instantly and refresh every 15 minutes. Any other ticker is looked up live by racing several public CORS proxies at once, since CBOE's chain endpoint isn't reachable from a browser directly. That path is best-effort and can occasionally run slow or fail outright if every proxy is down at the same time. Data: CBOE 15-minute delayed. Not investment advice.
