Insider Trades

Company insiders from SEC Form 4 filings, and members of Congress from House financial disclosures. Both are filterable, and every row links back to the source filing.

Company Insiders come from SEC EDGAR, Form 4 filings only, meaning officers, directors, and 10% owners. Covered names carry full transaction detail, including the insider, transaction type, share count, and price, parsed and refreshed periodically. Any other ticker loads live from EDGAR as a filing list with links, since the SEC does not permit browsers to read the transaction XML directly, so detail for uncovered tickers opens on EDGAR itself. Transaction codes: P buy, S sell, A grant, F tax withholding, M option exercise, G gift. Congress comes from the official House Clerk financial-disclosure index, covering Periodic Transaction Reports only. The House publishes no machine-readable transaction detail, so the linked PDF report is the source of truth for what was actually bought or sold. Senate disclosures aren't covered here; those live at efdsearch.senate.gov. All filings are delayed by SEC and House reporting deadlines. Not investment advice.
