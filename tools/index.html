Market Tools

Market structure tools and metrics, open to anyone. No signup, no paywall, just the data.

Gamma Exposure (GEX) OptionsDealer Positioning Net dealer gamma by strike. Contract selection runs on its own, pulling the expirations and strikes that actually carry open interest, then marking the zero-gamma flip, the call wall, and the put wall. Any optionable ticker is searchable. Open GEX → Options Greeks Lab OptionsPricing Model A Black-Scholes calculator with dividend yield: premium, all five greeks, a solver that inverts a market price back into implied volatility, and payoff versus value curves. Every unit convention is written out on the page. Open Greeks Lab → Macro Dashboard SentimentFed PolicyInflation A fear-to-complacency gauge, a Fed regime matrix built on rate level and balance-sheet trend, plus inflation, the yield curve, and liquidity. Everything is sourced from CBOE and FRED. Open Macro → Insider Trades SEC Form 4Congress Company insider buys and sells from SEC Form 4, plus congressional stock trades from House disclosures. Both are filterable by side, size, and name, and every row links back to the filing it came from. Open Insiders →
Volatility Surface & Skew OptionsImplied Volatility The implied volatility smile by expiration and the at-the-money term structure, built from out-of-the-money quotes only. Skew is normalized into standard deviations so it stays comparable across expirations, and the 30-day level is interpolated in variance time. Coming Soon
Smart-Money Signals CompositePositioning One read per ticker on where informed money is leaning, built from insider buying on Form 4, near-the-money options positioning, and the dealer gamma regime, combined into a single composite score and radar. Any covered name is searchable. Coming Soon

Data is delayed and published for informational and educational purposes only. Nothing here is investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Option data comes from CBOE on a 15-minute delay. The Greeks Lab is different: it computes everything locally in the browser from manually entered inputs and sends nothing anywhere. Macro data comes from FRED, insider data from SEC EDGAR, and congressional data from the House Clerk. Every prefetched feed refreshes on its own. Figures may still carry errors or gaps.
